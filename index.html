How to play
  • The white box follows your cursor. When a red dashed box snaps onto a nearby tile, that is the tile your click will hit — you do not need pixel-perfect aim.
  • Click any lit-up tile bordering your territory to open an offensive there — your troops then push across that whole contact line on their own, tick after tick.
  • The attack slider sets what share of your troop pool you commit; click again on the same rival to reinforce that offensive.
  • Troops regrow each tick based on how much land — and what kind — you hold.
  • Darker tiles are mountains: slower to grow, tougher to take.
  • Your troops can storm a shore across a narrow strait, so an island nation can still expand — it just costs about twice as much as a land border.
  • Rival bots expand and fight on their own. Control 60% of the land to win.
  • Naval warfare: build a port, commission warships for defence, then launch a naval invasion to send a transport to distant islands and establish a beachhead.
  • Camera: mouse wheel or +/− to zoom, drag the map to pan, and press 0 to reset the view. The interface can be hidden with the ◉ button. Your name grows with territory; ♛ marks the largest empire.
